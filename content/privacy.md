## 5. Security reports

If you report a vulnerability through the contact listed in our [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt), we use
your report and contact details to investigate and fix the issue and to keep you informed. The legal basis is our
legitimate interest in securing our systems (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep reports as long as needed to resolve and
document the issue, and we name you publicly only with your permission.

## 6. Links to other websites

Our pages contain plain links to other websites, such as those of public authorities. No data is sent to those
websites until you click a link. After that, the other website's own privacy policy applies.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15)
- have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
- have your data erased (Art. 17)
- restrict how we process it (Art. 18)
- receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
- object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular
  situation (Art. 21)

To exercise these rights, write to [info@mamorulab.com](mailto:info@mamorulab.com). You also have the right to
lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42,
1030 Wien, [dsb@dsb.gv.at](mailto:dsb@dsb.gv.at), [www.dsb.gv.at](https://www.dsb.gv.at).

## 9. Other information

You are not obliged to give us any personal data, but without your contact details we cannot reply to you. We do
not use automated decision-making or profiling. This website is delivered only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
